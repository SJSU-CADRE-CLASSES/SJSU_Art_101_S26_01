Animated Short

These concept drawings are from an animated short I’ve been working on called “The Little Viking Girl.” The story follows a Viking girl named Mia who is trying to hit a fly in her home but keeps failing, which leads to a series of funny and unexpected events. The short is filled with playful action scenes and comedic moments that capture Mia’s determination and clumsy charm. I originally came up with the idea back in 2019 when I was taking an animation class, inspired by the challenge of creating humor and movement in a small, everyday scenario. While the idea had been in my mind for a few years, I only began working seriously on the animatic in October 2024. Working on the animatic has been a fun and rewarding process, as I’ve been able to experiment with timing, composition, and character expressions to make the story engaging and lively. In addition to the concept drawings for my animated short, I’ve included some of my acrylic and oil paintings, as well as a few graphic designs, as a creative supplement to showcase different aspects of my artistic practice. My most recent painting depicts me and my little sister when we were kids. The ocean painting was created during the pandemic, and the fruit painting dates back to middle school and highlights my early exploration of color, texture, and form. The graphic designs included were created for a club application some time ago, and I was happy with how they turned out. I find that experimenting across different mediums – animation, painting, and digital design – helps me develop a broader understanding of visual storytelling, composition, and creativity. Each medium allows me to explore different ideas and express myself in unique ways, whether it’s through movement in animation, brushwork in painting, or fun layouts in graphic design. Together, these works reflect my ongoing journey as an artist and my passion for creating stories, images, and designs that are both personal and engaging.
