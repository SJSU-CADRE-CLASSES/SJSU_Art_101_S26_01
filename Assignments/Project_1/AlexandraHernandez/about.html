Concept

Strikes & Data treats bowling as a lens for self-inquiry. What can we learn when we reimagine the lane and score as a database?

Bowling is rarely seen as data-rich. This project flips that: the lane becomes a stage for measurement, the score a story.

Home: Interactive pins. Every roll produces data. Champions: PBA & PWBA stats—titles, majors, earnings. Filter and hover to explore. Journey: A fictional bowler's 6-year timeline. Click years to see persistence in numbers.

Interaction is central. The visualization asks: How does bowling—as sport, ritual, record—reflect who we are? Data is not separate from story. It is the story.
