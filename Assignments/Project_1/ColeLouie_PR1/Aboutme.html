For this webpage, I wanted my digital portrait to be about my career interests and projections growing up and I wanted to showcase them in a unique and fun way considering that this is an odd take about my life. What I have come up with for this undertaking is by having an interactive "what if" scenario if I never became a graphic designer. The thought of the project was if you, the viewer, were asking the one question to my alternate versions of myself saying "Aren't you supposed to be a graphic designer?" to which the site would reply "A graphic designer? That doesn't sound like me, I'm actually a _____". This prompt was meant to loop around infinitely to have fluidity throughout the project as well as add some multiversal fun to the fact that there are infinite possibilities to what I could be.

The designing of the project was to have all the themes of career options vastly different from each other in look and interactivity but having the main page be a completely blank slate. The themes heavily reflect with the career selection such as the marine biology looking like an old sailor's tale while the comic looks like something out of a small biographical zine. The main page was left blank because I wanted it to represent a blank canvas and a simple "meeting" space as you meet the alternate versions of myself and pick the little adventure.

When creating the alternate versions, I wanted to go with two main interests and one completely made up one to play around with the code and see how far I was able to take it. The interactive portion of the site is very impressive yet very simple and reflect the tone of stories that are being told.
