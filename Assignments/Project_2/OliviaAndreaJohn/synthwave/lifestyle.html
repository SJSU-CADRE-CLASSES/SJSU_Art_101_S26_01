Step into a synthwave penthouse rendered in realtime neon. This 3D space is a playable slice of daily life in the district: a glass‑walled lounge hovering over an endless grid, with plasma pillars, glowing plants, and a skyline of looping sunset bands.

Move through the mansion the way residents drift through the night: slow, deliberate, and surrounded by color. The room tracks your position, shifting light as you cross from cyan‑cooled corners to magenta‑hot vistas. Look around, line up with the balcony, and imagine the low thrum of retro‑futurist basslines echoing off mirrored glass.

This is not just décor; it is an interface to the lifestyle itself. Every surface hums with low‑power lumens, furniture floats just above the floor grid, and the mansion acts as a personal observatory over the endless synthwave horizon.
