Sector 03: Vaporwave District

A melancholy utopia built from obsolete dreams.

The Vaporwave District is where the utopia begins to feel a little too perfect. Neon storefronts loop the same sale animations forever, elevators hum the same reverb‑drenched jazz, and the sunsets are locked in a permanent screenshot.

Here, every surface is a remix of early internet UI: floating windows, plastic gradients, stock‑photo palm trees, and chrome word art selling experiences that no longer exist offline. Residents drift through arcades and food courts that feel like half‑remembered dreams of abandoned malls.

In this Wave, skeuomorphism becomes comfort. Interfaces pretend to be old hardware: chunky MP3 players, CRT security feeds, glowing shop directories. The government uses this nostalgia to keep visitors calm, replaying memories of a future that never arrived while taxing every micro‑transaction and vending‑machine wish.
