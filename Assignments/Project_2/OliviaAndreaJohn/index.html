The Future Waves

About our Page: (Click Me)

Our website exists in a distant future world based entirely on Internet aesthetics, most notably Frutiger Aero, Synthwave, and Vaporwave. The world is a utopia, depicting the most ideal version of the future, but if it were based solely on these aforementioned aesthetics. This plays into the main ideas of these aesthetics, especially Frutiger Aero. Frutiger Aero was heavily rooted in ideas of a utopic future.

Our website would reflect ideas of skeuomorphism, where new technologies emulate older technologies. This would be a recurring theme throughout the different pages, because even though our world exists in the distant future, the technology would emulate what would be considered older or maybe even obsolete technology.

Our website starts with a blank, nondescript homepage. This is called The Wave Surfer. You click a link to whatever Wave you would like to explore, and it takes you to a page that tells you all about that Wave. There would be beautiful, vibrant images of what the world looks like, in an effort to sell you on the idea of spending time in that specific wave (sector of the world). From here, visitors have the option to “teleport” to any of the three worlds and have a unique experience in each one.

The Waves and the Wave Surfer are controlled by a benevolent governmental force. The government generates revenue through taxing the vendors that live in the Waves. Therefore, the government tries to sell you on the idea of living in this perfect utopia so they can get your money, essentially.
